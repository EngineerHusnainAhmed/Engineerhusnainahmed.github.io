Design and Control of Ball Balancing System

A closed-loop platform that keeps a ball at a chosen point on a resistive touch panel. Three NEMA 17 stepper motors tilt the plate through a 3RPS parallel manipulator, while a PID controller reacts to the ball's measured position.

What the system does


Balancing a ball is an unstable problem: the ball accelerates down any slope, so the platform has to keep correcting its tilt.




01 — Sense
Touch-panel feedback
A resistive touch screen reports the ball's X and Y position. When no ball is detected, the platform returns to its home height.


02 — Decide
PID control
Separate X and Y loops compare the ball's position to the target and produce a tilt command every 20 ms.


03 — Act
Three-leg actuation
Inverse kinematics converts the tilt into three motor angles, and the steppers move the plate with speed scaled to the position error.







Controller and firmware


Every 20 ms the firmware reads the ball position, computes the error on each axis and updates the platform tilt.

Mechanism and electronics


A 3RPS parallel manipulator gives the plate two tilt axes and height from three actuated legs.




Mechanism3RPS parallel manipulator, three legs at 120°


Actuators3 × NEMA 17 stepper motors, 16 microsteps (3200 steps per revolution)


SensorResistive touch panel read through the TouchScreen library


ControllerArduino, with AccelStepper and MultiStepper for motion control


Link lengthsBase radius 2.000 in · arm 1.750 in · rod 3.125 in · platform radius 3.669 in


DebuggingSerial output at 115200 baud (X/Y output and motor speed)
