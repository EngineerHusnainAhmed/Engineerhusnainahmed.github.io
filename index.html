02 — Final year project

Design and Control of Ball Balancing System

A platform that keeps a ball at a target point on a resistive touch panel. Three NEMA 17 stepper motors tilt the plate through a 3RPS parallel manipulator, and a PID controller reads the ball's position every 20 ms to correct its tilt.
